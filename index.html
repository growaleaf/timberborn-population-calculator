Timberborn — Population Growth & Breeding Projection Calculator

Enter your colony's current population, food surplus, water surplus and breeding setup from Timberborn's own overview panel and see a projected population timeline, with a clear warning for the day your entered surplus runs negative if the population you're about to grow keeps growing. Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere; the math runs in your browser.

Correction to the brief this tool was built from The brief's input list ("number of Breeding Pods") describes the Iron Teeth faction only. Folktails don't build Breeding Pods — they breed naturally in housing, and no source found this session publishes a per-day breeding probability for that mechanic, so a day-by-day growth curve for Folktails would be fabricated. This page covers Iron Teeth with a full projected timeline (fully sourced, deterministic), and Folktails with the official steady-state formula instead of an invented curve — see the Folktails panel and Sources below for why.

Sources & last verified date

All figures below fetched directly 2026-10-09. Every numeric constant is checked against a second independent source where one exists; where it doesn't, that's stated below and the field is editable rather than hidden.

  1. timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Breeding_Pod — Breeding Pod costs 10 Logs, consumes 5 Water + 5 Berries per 5-day cycle to produce 1 kit. timberborn.org/articles/breeding-reproduction-guide corroborates the mechanic ("require berries and water to operate") but gives no cycle length or quantities, so the exact numbers are single-sourced to wiki.gg.
  2. timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Breeding — Iron Teeth pod capacity formula k = Amax/5 with the well-being-band table used on this page (10/12/14/16/18/20/21/22 beavers per pod across bands 1-6 through 67+); the Folktails equilibrium ratio lifeExpectancy / 5, with the page's own worked example ("at a life expectancy of 60 days, there will be 1 kit for every 12 adults") reproduced exactly by this page's math (see test.js, Scenario C). Single-sourced — timberborn.org does not publish either formula.

Conflict, not corroborated: wiki.gg's breeding formula uses "5" explicitly described as "the number of days for kits to mature," but timberborn.org/wiki/beaver-life-stages/kit states "Kits mature into Adults after 6 days" at base Growth Speed (3-4 days to longer, depending on well-being). Per this lane's sourcing rule, an unresolved conflict ships as a player-editable field, not a fabricated single value — see "Kit maturation (days)" above, defaulted to wiki.gg's 5 because that is the value the capacity formula itself is built on.

  5. timberborn.wiki.gg/wiki/Thirst — "Beavers drink between 2 and 3 units of Water a day," derived precisely as "-70/day / 33 per water consumed = 2.121 units of water a day on average," corroborated by timberborn.org's "approximately 2.1... 2.25 water per beaver per day" planning figure. This page uses 2.1.
